# 🌙 Nocturnal Instinct

> Survival isn't just about managing resources—it's about outsmarting predators that learn and adapt to how you play.

**Nocturnal Instinct** is a tactical survival game developed in **Godot Engine**. Face the harsh realities of a full day/night cycle, manage your health and scarce resources, and survive against intelligent enemies whose behavior evolves based on your strategic choices.

---

## 🎮 Key Features

* **Resource & Health Management:** Monitor your vitals and gather limited resources to stay alive.
* **Dynamic Day/Night Cycle:** The environment, visibility, and threats shift dramatically as day turns to night.
* **Adaptive AI Enemies:** Opponents don't just follow a script—they analyze your playstyle and alter their hunting strategies to counter you.
* **Built with Godot:** Developed using modern GDScript architecture, custom state machines, and efficient node structures.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools

* **Engine:** Godot Engine (4.x)
* **Language:** GDScript
* **Version Control:** Git & GitHub
